## Monday: Sesame Chicken + Rice
*Servings: 2 | Prep: 10m | Cook: 10m*

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb chicken breast (sous vide)
- 0.25 tsp ginger powder
- Salt and black pepper
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil (divided)
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 0.5 tsp red pepper flakes
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups cooked white rice
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- **Sauce:** 1/3 cup tamari, 3 tbsp honey, 2 tsp tomato paste, 2.5 tbsp rice vinegar, 0.5 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp sesame seeds, 1 tsp fresh grated ginger
- **Slurry:** 1 tbsp arrowroot + 0.25 cup water

**Instructions:**

PREP:
1. Remove sous vide chicken breast from bag and pat completely dry with paper towels.
2. Slice chicken into 1/2-inch strips or bite-sized pieces.
3. Season lightly with salt, pepper, and ginger powder.

MAKE THE SAUCE:
4. In a small bowl, whisk together: 1/3 cup tamari, 3 tbsp honey, 2 tsp tomato paste, 2.5 tbsp rice vinegar, 0.5 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp sesame seeds, 1 tsp fresh grated ginger.
5. In a separate small bowl, mix 1 tbsp arrowroot with 1/4 cup water to make a slurry. Set aside.

SEAR THE CHICKEN:
6. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat until very hot (2-3 minutes).
7. Add 1 tbsp avocado oil. When shimmering, add chicken pieces in a single layer.
8. Let sear without moving for 2 minutes until golden and crispy.
9. Flip pieces and sear another 1-2 minutes. Remove to a plate.

BUILD THE SAUCE:
10. Reduce heat to medium. Add 0.5 tbsp sesame oil to the pan.
11. Add red pepper flakes and minced garlic. Cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
12. Pour in half the sauce. Let it bubble for 30 seconds.
13. Return chicken to pan. Add remaining sauce and toss to coat.
14. Drizzle in the arrowroot slurry while stirring.
15. Cook 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and glazes the chicken.

SERVE:
16. Serve immediately over cooked rice.
17. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced scallions.

## Tuesday: Chilean Sea Bass + Asparagus + Mashed Potatoes
*Servings: 2 | Prep: 10m | Cook: 15m*

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb Chilean sea bass fillets
- 1 bunch asparagus
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon
- Salt and black pepper
- *Mashed potatoes (already prepared)*

**Instructions:**

PREP:
1. Remove sea bass from refrigerator 15 minutes before cooking.
2. Pat fish completely dry with paper towels.
3. Season with salt, pepper, and zest from half the lemon.
4. Trim woody ends from asparagus. If thick, cut in half lengthwise.
5. Mince the garlic.

COOK THE ASPARAGUS:
6. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in skillet over medium-high.
7. Add asparagus in single layer. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Cook without stirring 2-3 minutes until charred.
9. Stir and cook 2-3 more minutes until tender-crisp.
10. Add garlic in last 30 seconds. Transfer to plate.

SEAR THE SEA BASS:
11. Wipe skillet. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium-high.
12. Place sea bass presentation-side down.
13. Press gently with spatula for first 30 seconds.
14. Cook 3-4 minutes until golden crust forms.
15. Flip and cook 2-3 more minutes until opaque and flaky (130-135°F internal).

SERVE:
16. Place asparagus on plates, top with sea bass.
17. Squeeze remaining lemon over everything.
18. Serve with mashed potatoes.

## Wednesday: Honey Mustard Chicken + Cornbread + Mashed Potatoes
*Servings: 2 | Prep: 5m | Cook: 10m*

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb chicken breast (sous vide)
- 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- *Cornbread (already prepared)*
- *Mashed potatoes (already prepared)*

**Instructions:**

PREP THE SAUCE:
1. Whisk together Dijon mustard, honey, 1 tbsp olive oil, and minced garlic until smooth. Set aside.

SEAR THE CHICKEN:
2. Remove sous vide chicken from bag and pat completely dry with paper towels. This is crucial for a good sear.
3. Heat cast iron or stainless steel skillet over medium-high until very hot (about 2 minutes).
4. Add 1 tbsp olive oil. When it shimmers, add chicken breasts.
5. Sear without moving for 2-3 minutes until golden-brown crust forms.
6. Flip and sear other side for 2-3 minutes.

MAKE THE GLAZE:
7. Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour honey mustard sauce into pan around chicken.
8. Let sauce bubble and thicken slightly (30-60 seconds).
9. Spoon sauce over chicken repeatedly to coat and glaze.
10. Remove from heat when sauce is thick and glossy.

SERVE:
11. Let chicken rest 2 minutes, then slice if desired.
12. Drizzle remaining pan sauce over top.
13. Serve with cornbread and mashed potatoes.

## Thursday: Lebanese Quinoa Chicken Bowls
*Servings: 4 | Prep: 20m | Cook: 40m*

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb chicken breast (sous vide)
- 0.5 tsp cumin
- 0.5 tsp paprika
- Salt, olive oil
- 1.5 cups uncooked quinoa
- 0.25 cup pine nuts
- 8 oz halloumi, grated
- Kalamata olives, pitted and chopped
- Fresh mint and parsley
- Lemon wedges
- **Cucumber-Tomato Salad:** 4 Persian cucumbers, 1 pint cherry tomatoes, 1.5 tsp salt, 0.5 cup Greek yogurt, 1 lemon juiced

**Instructions:**

PREP THE SALAD:
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line baking sheet with parchment.
2. Smash cucumbers with knife, cut into bite-sized chunks.
3. Halve cherry tomatoes. Add to cucumbers.
4. Season with salt, set in colander to drain 30 minutes.

SEAR THE CHICKEN:
5. Remove sous vide chicken breast from bag, pat completely dry.
6. Season with cumin, paprika, and a pinch of salt.
7. Heat skillet over medium-high until very hot.
8. Add 1 tbsp olive oil. When shimmering, add chicken.
9. Sear 2-3 minutes per side until golden and crusty.
10. Rest 2 minutes, then slice into bite-sized pieces.

COOK THE QUINOA:
11. Rinse quinoa until water runs clear.
12. Add to pot with 2.25 cups water and 1.5 tsp salt.
13. Bring to boil, reduce to low, cover. Cook 15 minutes.
14. Turn off heat, steam 10 more minutes.

TOAST PINE NUTS:
15. Heat 1 tsp olive oil in small pan over medium.
16. Add pine nuts, stir frequently until golden (2-3 minutes). Transfer to bowl.

FINISH AND SERVE:
17. Mix drained cucumbers/tomatoes with Greek yogurt and lemon juice.
18. Divide quinoa between bowls.
19. Top with chicken, cucumber-tomato salad, halloumi.
20. Add pine nuts, olives, mint, parsley.
21. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Friday: Greek Chicken + Roasted Veggies
*Servings: 2 | Prep: 10m | Cook: 30m*

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb chicken breast (sous vide)
- 2 bell peppers
- 1 red onion
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 lemon
- Salt and black pepper

**Instructions:**

PREP THE VEGETABLES:
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line baking sheet with parchment.
2. Cut bell peppers into 1-inch chunks.
3. Cut red onion into 1-inch wedges, keeping root intact.
4. Halve cherry tomatoes.
5. Toss vegetables with 2 tbsp olive oil, oregano, 0.5 tsp salt, 0.25 tsp pepper.

ROAST THE VEGETABLES:
6. Spread vegetables in single layer on baking sheet.
7. Roast 15 minutes, then stir/flip.
8. Continue roasting 10-15 more minutes until charred and tender.
9. Tomatoes will burst - this is good!

SEAR THE CHICKEN (start when veggies have 10 min left):
10. Remove sous vide chicken from bag, pat completely dry.
11. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
12. Heat skillet over medium-high until very hot.
13. Add 1 tbsp olive oil. When shimmering, add chicken.
14. Sear 2-3 minutes per side until golden and crusty.
15. Rest 2 minutes, then slice against the grain.

SERVE:
16. Spoon roasted vegetables onto plates.
17. Arrange sliced chicken on top.
18. Squeeze lemon generously over everything.
19. Optional: drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with fresh oregano or feta.

## Lunch: Turkey Chili (2x batch)
*Servings: 16 | Prep: 10m | Cook: 4 hours*

**Ingredients (doubled):**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 lbs ground turkey
- 2 onions, diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 red bell peppers, diced
- 1.5 cups chicken broth
- 2 cans (15 oz) red kidney beans, drained
- 2 cans (15 oz) Great Northern beans, drained
- 2 cans (28 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cans (15 oz) tomato sauce
- 2 cans (4 oz) diced green chiles
- 5 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp cumin
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp sea salt
- 0.5-1.5 tsp cayenne (to taste)

**Instructions:**
1. Heat olive oil in large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add ground turkey, onions, and garlic. Sauté 5-7 minutes, breaking up turkey, until browned.
3. Transfer to slow cooker.
4. Add all remaining ingredients to slow cooker. Stir well.
5. Cook on HIGH 3-4 hours, or LOW 7-8 hours.
